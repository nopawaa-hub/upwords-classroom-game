How to Play UpWords Tryout!

1. Build Across or Down

Spell simple English words left-to-right or top-to-bottom (like BUS, CAT, PLAY, STAR).

2. Stack Letters on Top!

Put a new letter on top of an old letter to change the word! (Example: Stack G on S in BUS to make BUG!)

3. Green Stack Checker

Click "Help Here!" and tap any word on the board. A green "It Can Be Stacked!" box will show you easy hints and letter choices!

4. A–Z Letter Finder

Not sure how to spell a word? Tap 1 or 2 starting letters (like "S" "P") in the Dictionary tab to see a list of words!
